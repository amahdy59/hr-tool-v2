# Mobile experience review — 27 October 2026

## Changes

- Simplified mobile navigation to Home, Requests, Attendance, and More, with a single persistent request action and preferences available from More.
- Reserved layout space for the fixed navigation so record cards scroll into the usable content area. Kept target sizes, safe areas, logical RTL spacing, readable input sizes, and reduced motion support.
- Added a combined requests destination with search, type filters, loading, retry, empty results, detail views, a status donut with exact text counts, and a labeled progress diagram.
- Preserved destination state and scroll position across Back navigation. Back dismisses forms, preferences, and case-study screenshots before leaving their parent screen.
- Saved unfinished request drafts per account and form for the current tab session; cleared them after successful creation or logout. Attachments are explicitly excluded from draft restoration.
- Kept form headers and submission footers outside the scrolling field area, with sizing based on the visible viewport when the keyboard opens.
- Reflowed mobile payroll and assigned-holiday records into labeled rows and improved employee card wrapping.
- Moved demo access and credentials outside the login form card. Added problem/decision/evidence explanations, a four-step journey, screenshot enlargement, keyboard-operable comparison tabs, and explicit validation limits to the case study.

## Verification limits

Browser checks use Chromium/Edge with English and Arabic, both themes, touch emulation, reduced motion, keyboard interaction, and widths from 320px through desktop. The keyboard check simulates `visualViewport` resizing and checks the form against its visible bottom edge. Physical iOS/Android keyboards, device safe-area behavior, and spoken VoiceOver/TalkBack output require device testing. Automated axe results are scoped to the audited states and do not establish complete WCAG conformance. No measured business or task-completion outcomes are claimed.
